Under-the-radar engine

Candidates who do not dominate public lists but match the club's model unusually well.

Why they surface

Low media visibility, strong age curve, positive development slope, and fit against the club's internal benchmark.

How they are scored

No opaque talent score. Every candidate receives fit reasons, confidence values and clear caveats.

Decision safety

The system highlights uncertainty when data volume, opponent strength or video coverage is thin.

Scout role

Scout remains the final decision-maker, with the platform acting as a highly structured assistant.
