Analysis layers

A real system needs separate layers for detection, tracking, event extraction and tactical interpretation.

Detection

Locate players, ball and pitch reference points before building any tactical interpretation.

Tracking

Maintain sequence identity across frames, even with frequent occlusion or camera cuts.

Event extraction

Mark passes, pressures, recoveries, shots and transitions as explainable events.

Tactical interpretation

Convert movement patterns into line height, compactness, pressing structure and spacing signals.

Known limits

The model must explain what it cannot know from broadcast footage alone.

OcclusionSome players are hidden by the camera angle.
Camera heightHigh or low broadcast angles change spatial accuracy.
Frame qualityCompression and blur affect detection confidence.
Context missingWithout tracking data, some off-ball conclusions stay probabilistic.
